# Detailed Strategy Mapping: Homes NSW Business Capability Model vs. Strategy 2025–2035

# 1\. Strategic Alignment Framework and Methodology

This mapping provides a structural reconciliation between the Homes NSW Business Architecture Capability Model and the three core pillars of the *Homes for NSW Strategy 2025–2035*: **Customer-driven service**, **More and better homes**, and **A system that works**. From an architectural perspective, this transition represents a pivot from a transactional, rationing-based landlord model to a sophisticated, data-led "System Steward" posture.

The following demonstrates how every internal capability—from technical property compliance to high-level system stewardship—serves as an engine for the three core priorities of the Strategy:

1. **Customer-driven service:** Transitioning to a person-centred system that treats people with kindness, dignity, and respect.  
2. **More and better homes:** Managing the largest investment in social housing in the state’s history to deliver high-quality, sustainable infrastructure.  
3. **A system that works:** Leading as the "System Steward" to foster a viable, integrated multi-provider ecosystem.

The hierarchy follows a formal Domain (Level 1), Group (Level 2), and Capability (Level 3\) structure.
